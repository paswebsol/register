Class Scores

Enter up to five class assessment scores for each student and subject. Each assessment is out of 10; the five scores are added automatically to make the Class Score out of 50. Examination scores are entered on the separate Examination Scores page.

The five assessment scores are saved individually. Their sum is saved as the Class Score / 50 and used by student reports and rankings. Existing examination scores are preserved when you update class assessments.

Examination Scores

Enter the examination mark out of 100 for a student and subject. The app automatically converts it to 50% (out of 50). Class scores are entered on the separate Class Scores page.

Enter the raw examination mark from 1 to 100. The app converts it to a score out of 50 automatically (mark ÷ 2). Reports show the converted /50 score, and existing class scores are preserved.

Termly Student Academic Report

Generate a report for one student for a selected term. Each subject is calculated as Class Score / 50 + Examination Mark converted to /50 = Final Score / 100 (100%).

Choose a student and generate the report.

To save or share a PDF on Windows, MacBook, Chromebook, Android, iPhone or iPad: generate the report, choose Print / Save / Share PDF, then select Save as PDF (or PDF destination). Open the saved PDF to share it by email, messaging, AirDrop or another available sharing option. Enable background graphics / print backgrounds for best colour output.

How to Use School Bills

  1. Set prices (optional): Open Bill Pricing by Term, choose the term and bill type, enter the standard amount in GHS, then select Save / Update Price.
  2. Add a bill: In School Fees & Other Bills, select the student, bill type and term. Enter a description, amount, any discount or scholarship, and due date, then select Add Bill.
  3. Record money received: Under Record Student Payment, select the term, student and bill. Enter the amount actually received, choose the payment method, add a transaction reference if available, then select Record Payment & Generate Receipt.
  4. Save or print the receipt: In the print dialog, choose Save as PDF or print a paper copy.
  5. Check balances: Review Outstanding by Selected Term, Term Bill Summary, Student Financial Status, and Bills & Payment History.

Important: Adding a bill records what is owed; it does not record a payment. Record only the amount actually received. For a partial payment, enter the partial amount and record any later payment separately.
